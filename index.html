Quick guide

How compound interest works

Compound interest means you earn interest on your original money and on interest already earned. This calculator uses monthly compounding and assumes monthly contributions are added at the end of each month.

Common questions

Compound interest FAQ

What is compound interest?

It is interest earned on both your original balance and previously earned interest.

How often does this calculator compound interest?

The calculation compounds interest monthly and adds monthly contributions at the end of each month.

Is this result guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate for planning and does not include taxes, fees, or changing investment returns.
